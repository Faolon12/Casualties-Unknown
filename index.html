// Share yours

  1. Export from the editor (rig zip, add-on, gear or props pack), or zip your sprites.
  2. Take a picture of it: a screenshot from the editor's Test tab or the game works great.
  3. Press the button and fill in the form. You'll need a free GitHub account. Drag the zip and the picture into the form.
  4. Once it's checked, it shows up here and you get a comment on your post.
Share something ↗ Share a stream setup code ↗

Only share things you made, or have permission to share. No content from the game's late layers in pictures or names, so nobody gets spoiled. Zips may only contain .png, .json, .wav, .ogg, .mp3, .txt and .md files.
